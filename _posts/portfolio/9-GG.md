An integral part of my college experience so far has been participating in the
Garman Guides program at Bucknell. First year engineering students across all
majors must take an audit class one evening a week that is student-taught. This
class focuses on topics decided by faculty oversight, ranging from test prep to
comfort and life on campus. My experience during my freshman year really helped
me to adjust to life in Lewisburg and meet people in the college as well as my
department.

Near the end of my freshman year, I was offered the opportunity to become one
of these student teachers: a Garman Guide. I wanted to make the same impact that
I felt on my peers; give them in turn a place to express fears, ask questions,
and familiarize themselves with the ECE department. Over the past two years I've
had two great groups of first-year students: some of them continue to be my
friends, coworkers, and friendly faces around campus. I feel that I've really
been given the opportunity to make a difference in our community in a positive
way, and I continue to be thankful for the opportunity. Pictured is our group
of Guides at our annual training. Learn more about the Garman Guides program
in [this article about one of my Baja peers.](https://www.bucknell.edu/news/garman-guides-social-safety-net-new-engineers)
