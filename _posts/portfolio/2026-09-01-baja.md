SAE Baja is a national competition by the Society of Automotive Engineers. It's the sister competition of Formula Student: Baja is based off of the rally that occurs
in Baja, California. Teams of students from universities across the US compete to design and manufacture the most capable off-road race vehicle they can within certain
guidelines.

While this competition mainly attracts mechanical engineers, there are some electronics on the car that warrant a subsection of the team focused on ECE subjects. During the
2024-25 academic year, I led a group of ~10 Electrical or Computer Engineers in order to create a robust system on the Bucknell Baja car: these include a datalogger, controls
for electronic differentials, and a wiring harness responsible for engine management and killswitches. Part of my role on this team was delegating tasks to those on our
subteam, while contributing significantly to our products myself.

During the 2025-2026 academic year, my responsibilities evolved to become more of an individual
contributor to allow others to gain some leadership experience. With still a very small
team, my contributions ended up being focused on 3D modeling and materials considerations
to create an effective new steering wheel for the car. After some attempts using glass
fiber filament, the final 3D printed wheel used a sheet of 1/4in aluminum inside for
reinforcement. During this time and over the following summer, in my free time I focused
on creating a driver feedback system, integrating an MCU into a screen module to have
some minor sensor feedback directly to the driver as well as possible drive modes selectable
by the driver.

I had the chance to attend the 2026 Baja SAE competition in Rochester, NY, the only competition
the Bucknell team was able to attend. We accomplished an overall position of 45th out of 102 teams in attendance.
Notably, we finished the entire 4 hour endurance race with a position of 35th! This is the first time
the Bucknell team has accomplished this in close to a decade. If you'd like to learn more, please read [this article from Bucknell](https://www.bucknell.edu/news/bucknell-baja-racing-team-takes-custom-built-car-regional-competition).
